Problem

Wax, fragrance and essential oils, containers, packaging, shipping and gifting materials all move through The Calm Flame with no map of where material use and waste actually occur — so there's no basis yet for redesigning the business around circularity.

Solution

Map the full product lifecycle, then run it through the Ellen MacArthur Foundation's Circular Design tools and intervention worksheets to identify concrete, costed changes rather than general sustainability language.
